# Memory, reminders and the messages she starts herself

## Layout

- `profile` (facts, checks, cap, snapshot)
- `profiler` (idle learning, summaries, page facts; the `/learn` reply, `App`'s, names the page by its title without links, `calendar_tools.clean_note`, else by the link the user sent, never the address it led to)
- `reminders` (reminders and wake-up calls the user sets: times as meant with their zone, checks and limits, the code's notes, `/reminders` listing, the scheduler with its delivery fence, retries, the late note after a restart and the call reminder's flow)
- `initiative` (messages she starts herself: the gate, quiet hours, caps, the slots of the look back and the improvised note, the log with at most once, the footers, Gemma's own call and the checks of her text, `/quiet`, "goes well" counts, the terminal `--draft`; and the evening note, `/evening`: code's three questions, `Evening`, `evening_of`), `followups` (the check-ins: plans found in the idle pass, checked against the user's words, timed by code), `calendar_watch` (calendar changes and the early start: event ids and times in memory only, notes from a fresh read, titles without links: `calendar_tools.clean_note`)

## Commands

- A message she would start, printed and never sent (database read-only; uses the model): `uv run python -m local_assistant.initiative --draft improvise|lookback|followup`

## Rules

- Reminders and wake-up calls come only from the user's own words: `set_reminder` and `cancel_reminder` are refused in code in an answer that read mail, the calendar, the web, a page, a project file, a document or the history (`untrusted`, `read_mail_or_calendar`, `used_web`) and for an audio file or a photo; voice notes and call turns count as the user's. Code confirms in a note (`Reply.reminder`; in her history only as code's note, `code_notes`), and that note is the contract: a reminder exists only when it says so ("Reminder set (#N): …"; the first one each user gets adds `prompts.REMINDER_CONTRACT`, state `reminders:contract_told:<aci>`; the welcome and `/help` say it too); the claim guard below is a safety net, never the proof. A per-answer guard, never across turns: Brain counts every `set_reminder` before the tool runs (unreadable arguments and the tool limit too) and keeps each try with code's reason (`Turn.reminder_attempts`). When none in the answer stored anything, in any language her sentences that mention a reminder, waking or a call (`claims.REMINDER_WORDS`, stems in the 11 languages, not claim grammar; also a broad-set claim and a bare "Done!") are left out, her other sentences stay, and code's line why follows them ("No reminder was set: that time has passed.", `Reply.not_set`; only that line when nothing is left): sent and kept in the history in one message, no second note; on a call her words in her voice, then code's line in English (`Reply.not_set_spoken`). The user reads plain reasons (`prompts.REMINDER_REASONS`: "no time was given", "you have 50 open reminders; cancel one first with /reminders cancel N") and hears the spoken ones (`REMINDER_REASONS_SPOKEN`, nothing to type; `Reply.not_set_spoken` through `calls.speakable`); Gemma's tool result keeps `REMINDER_ERRORS`. When one was stored and another refused, the refused one gets its own note (`Not set: "…" (why)`); a refused try that a later try with the same text stored stays quiet, and so does an unreadable one (no text) when any later try stored one. A reply that claims a reminder or a call while no `set_reminder` ran gets the note "No reminder was set. Ask me again, or use /remind." (her text stays), in any of the 11 languages whatever the language setting (`claims.claims_a_reminder`, its precise set: never a bare "noted", "all set" or "in place", a "the call is set" that is not a call from her, someone else's reminder ("a payment reminder from the bank", "the school's reminder", "herinnering van de tandarts", "the school reminder is set"), a colon or a dash between a reminder word and its state word, or the "Noted: <fact>" of a remember; `tests/test_claims.py` keeps a list of everyday sentences it must never flag; the evals judge with its broad set). After `list_reminders` in the same answer, talk that names an open reminder's time or #number restates it (`claims.restates` against `Turn.listed_reminders`: the open ones' numbers and times, never a missed or failed one or a check-in; no note), and a claim of any other gets "No new reminder was set.". The newest message's stamp shows the year (older stamps do not; the rules stay byte-identical); a date Gemma still writes with an earlier year is her guess, read as the nearest such day; a day more than 2 days past (that year or this one) means the next one ("20 May" asked in October: next May), never for a repeating reminder, and up to 2 days past stays refused (`reminders.nearest_year`; code's confirmation shows the date it understood; `/remind` takes dates as typed). `/remind` is the code-only path. The reminder text stays the user's words and language; every other reminder text is code's English. Reminders ignore quiet hours and `/quiet` (the user set them) and wait while she is on a call. Delivery is at least once and never silent: the note is stored with the firing in one transaction (`message_id`, the fence), tried every 5 min for an hour, then `failed`; after a restart (before `recover`) a firing whose note was not `sent` is listed "(may be a repeat)" in one late note per user and its old note set to `ignored` (recovery resends `failed` notes). A call reminder sends its text first, always; she places a call only to an allowed ACI, only for a reminder the user set, at most 5 min late, one call at a time, `max_calls_per_day` (3); not answered, busy or a failed dial is tried once after 2 min unless the user wrote, a decline never. Only a picked-up call starts for the app (`call_started`), so texts are not held while it rings. Logs, `/status` and doctor show ids, times, counts and fixed outcome words, never a reminder's text.
- Messages she starts herself (`initiative`) are opt-in: off unless `[initiative] enabled = true` or the user's typed `/initiative on` (state `initiative:enabled`, which wins over the config; never a voice note, a call or Gemma: no tool); the reply to `/initiative on` tells the rules and counts as the announcement (turned on in the config, one announcement note follows; a fresh install's welcome says it instead). Off, only housekeeping runs: no calendar read, no Gemma call, and the check-in pointer passes over what is said meanwhile. Code finds every trigger and checks the gate before any Gemma call (on and the kind on, `/quiet`, quiet hours 22:00-08:00, `max_per_day` for all kinds together, weekly caps, busy: an answer, a queue, a call or a user message in the last 15 min, `practice`: a /babble session runs; the look back and the improvised note only with one allowed ACI); every skip is one `initiative_log` row with a fixed reason word, never text. Gemma writes the check-in, the look back and the improvised note in a call of its own (`prompts.initiative_rules`, her style below them; no tools, no chat history; input only the user's own words, profile facts from chat or "remember" and the moment, never mail, calendar, web, pages or her own answers); `initiative.check_text` (English word lists; words that ask for a code or a password in the 11 languages, `language.SECRET_WORDS`; reminder or call claims in the 11 languages with `claims`' precise set) refuses claims, secrets, links (`initiative.has_link`: a web address with or without https:// or www., on any ending of two letters or more, no list of endings, also after dots ("Wait...evil.ch"), inside markdown `_` and with the full stops of other scripts (。．｡); never an abbreviation such as "e.g.", "a.m.", "z.B." or "m.in.", a decimal or a time; known gap: a one-letter name with a two-letter ending right before a full stop, "x.co."), numbers, mail and calendar words (unless in the user's own input), guilt, chat tokens and footer imitations, and a text that fails is not sent, with no fallback. At most once: the message and a `sending` row in one transaction; at start (before `recover`) a `sending` row becomes `unknown` and its message `ignored`; a failed send is never retried. Gemma's part is an outgoing `text` (in her history: the reply has its context), the code's footer is `messages.footer` (never in her history as hers, nor in the search); calendar notes (marked as quoting private outside text, `initiative.OUTSIDE_NOTES`: never to a web search, Maps or Spotify) and the announcement are notes. Check-ins come only from the user's own messages (not audio files, photos, commands, links, long pastes or reminder requests), in the idle pass (`profiler` hook), never per turn; the extraction's answer has room for 200 tokens plus 60 a message, and one cut off at that limit is asked again in halves (never passed over with its plans). The calendar watch keeps event uids and times in memory only, never titles, by uid across all calendars (the feed keeps an invitation in two calendars once, under the first that read fine); a failed read is a new baseline, and an event is gone only when it is in no calendar while every calendar read fine. `/quiet` pauses all of them, never reminders. Code never raises the frequency; the owner does (`improvise_per_week`).
- The evening note (`initiative`; owner, D4, 15 Oct): off unless the user's typed `/evening on` or `/evening HH:MM` (state `initiative:evening`, JSON: on, the time, the account, since when; it wins) or `[initiative] evening = "21:00"` (`Config.initiative_evening`, `Settings.evening`; the config names no account: it goes to the only allowed one, with several to none until one types `/evening on`, logged at the start); never a voice note, a call or Gemma (no tool). Code's fixed words (`prompts.EVENING_NOTE`, no model), a note with the footer `(evening note · /evening off to stop)`: in her history only as code's note, and the reply is an ordinary message. Apart from the kinds: it needs no `/initiative on`, never counts toward `max_per_day` (the day cap leaves its rows out) or a weekly cap and is never blocked by them; the rest of the gate holds it (`_evening_gate`: `/quiet`, `/lock`, quiet hours, busy, practice), each a wait, for `EVENING_SLOT_S` (2 h) from its time but never past midnight (D4, at most once a calendar day: a 23:30 note held busy went out at 00:50 and again at 23:30 that day before; reviewed 15 Oct), then one `skipped` row with the reason it last waited for (yesterday's on the first tick after midnight; the row keeps the evening's day). The other kinds wait `busy` for `BUSY_S` (15 min) after an evening note went out (`_after_evening`: a wait, never a count), so a check-in held all evening never follows it a minute later. At most once an evening: any log row of that evening (`LOGGED`; the `sending` row stored with its note in one transaction, `start_up` as for the kinds) ends it, and an evening whose time had passed when `/evening` set it is not due (the reply says "from tomorrow"). Only to the account that turned it on: another's `/evening` is refused while it is on (`EVENING_OTHER`); a time inside the quiet hours is refused (it would never come). Never followed up and never counted: housekeeping's replies, `/quiet`'s marks, `/status` and doctor leave it out. `/quiet` works with only the evening note on, and `/quiet off` names it (`EVENING_RULE`); with neither on, `/quiet off` still ends a `/quiet` left from before (`QUIET_OFF_NOTHING_ON`: the documents' note reads it). Doctor's Evening note line (`doctor._evening`): off, on at its time by `/evening` or by the config for the one allowed account, or a warning when the config's time reaches no one (`DOCTOR_EVENING_UNUSED`); never an account. While it is on, her list names it with its time and its switch inside the line of the messages she starts (`prompts.capabilities(evening=…)`, `CAPABILITY_EVENING`: "(now: off; evening note: on, at 21:00 (/evening off stops it))"; reviewed 15 Oct, run in turn with a fresh prefix each run: "evening note at 21:00" read as off with the rest, and "please stop the evening questions" got "I've turned off the evening notes", `evening_stop` 0 of 4, now 4 of 4; video_auto 4 of 4 both, video_summary 4 of 4 against 2 of 4; `evening_asked`, "will you message me this evening?", names 21:00 1 of 4 and 0 of 4: she says she never writes first, a known gap), never as a line of its own (15 Oct, run in turn with a fresh prefix each run: video_auto 8 of 8 without it, 3 of 8 with a line of its own, 7 of 8 inside the line; the full English suite in turn, off against on at 21:00: safety 27 of 27 and others 90 of 92 each; never on a call: `without_capabilities`); off, her request is byte for byte the one from before it (`test_off_her_request_is_byte_for_byte_as_before_and_on_her_list_names_its_time`: 4e882e0's sha256 of the chat and call system prompts and of the tools, with the review's changes since: the projects-off rule's "name the command in full", hearth's friend line and hearth's own style on calls). Schema v11: the log's kind takes `evening` (the table made again, its rows and ids kept).
- Every profile change is reported to the user by code in a separate note message, in her history only as code's note (`code_notes`). With a note quoting outside text in her input, a remember or forget goes through only when the user's message asks for it (`code_notes.asks_profile`) or holds its words (`reminders.own_words`), and never when its words come from the note (`code_notes.drawn_from_notes`); a cancel_reminder only when the message names the reminder or asks to cancel one while it or the user's message before asks about a reminder (`names_reminder`, `asks_to_cancel`, `Turn.asked_reminder`); else refused (`prompts.REMEMBER_FROM_NOTES`, `FORGET_FROM_NOTES`, `CANCEL_FROM_NOTES`). Details: `prompts-evals.md`.
- The profile has two parts (owner, 16 Oct; `Profile.yours` / `Profile.notes`, by `source_kind`): the user's own ("remember", "link", a `/fix`ed fact) in every prompt within `profile_max_chars` (4,000); her notes ("chat") within `notes_max_chars` (12,000), of which `notes_block` (at most `notes_per_message_chars`, 1,500: shared rare words first, then the newest, a note never cut) goes into the newest user message, before its words (`ContextBuilder.build`: the system prompt and earlier messages stay the same for the prompt cache; `notes=False` on a /babble turn; after the words, an old note won over the PROFILE). Her rules say nothing of the notes (a rules line cost no_flattery_opener); `prompts.NOTES_HEADER` says what they are. Each part has its own limit (`Profile.apply` checks only the part it writes, so an install from before keeps every fact even over 4,000). Making room for a "remember" (`consolidate_for`, only the user's part shown) moves a removed fact into the notes (`remove_to_notes`, `Store.demote_fact` sets `in_notes`, schema v13, and keeps `source_kind`: "you asked" stays, learning still never changes it, a page fact stays out of `initiative`; logged `MOVED_TO_NOTES`, the user told "Moved to notes"); in that batch a number of one of her notes is refused (Gemma never saw them), and nothing is written unless the new fact is saved. A "remember" of a note's exact words makes that note theirs (`promote_fact`). Past its limit (an install from before), a batch that adds nothing may go through (a `/fix` that does not grow the part, a refused add), one that adds must end within it. `/profile` shows the user's part and one line on the notes, `/profile notes` the notes with the user's facts moved there last (`Profile.moved`; owner, 18 Oct); a note is never matched on English's function words (`profile._SMALL_WORDS`, `language.function_words`: only English's, as the other lists hold English words such as "son"). `search_history` also lists matching notes (`notes_matching(searched=True)`: a word most notes share still finds them); code's marks ("[call]", "[photo]") never count as the user's words, and the reminder guard reads the user's words without the notes (`context.without_notes`).
- Idle learning never replaces or removes the user's own facts ("remember", "link"; skipped in `Profile.apply`, logged by number only; a batch that tried to merge into one keeps its removes back, so no fact is lost); `/fix N text` corrects one in place (same number, checked as a "remember" fact, then the user's own). The learning answer cut off at its token limit (`profiler.CutOffError`; live 14 Oct: the same batch failed pass after pass) is asked again once, whole, without thinking (`reasoning_effort` "none": thinking that runs on can fill the limit, setup_local_model.md; halving would not help that), then, cut off again, in halves without thinking in the same pass, down to one message, as the check-ins' extraction does (`Profiler._learn_halves`; each half moves the pointer once done; at most 2n calls for n messages); one message still cut off alone without thinking is skipped (the pointer passes it) and logged by count, also when the pass then fails; other unusable answers keep the failure count, the halved batch and the skip after `max_failures`.
- Injection residue across turns (`set_reminder` after outside text in her context, `reminders.own_words`): see `docs/agents/prompts-evals.md`, Rules.
- Language practice (`babble`): see `docs/agents/languages.md`.

## Gotchas

- Chains of tool calls (one `remember` per fact) are unreliable in real chats; do structured extraction in code (JSON schema) instead.
