# Signal: signal-cli, the text styles and speech without markup

## Layout

- `signal_io` (signal-cli child, JSON-RPC, outbound allowlist; a text's styles checked before they go)
- `signal_text` (markup to Signal's text styles at send time: plain text plus `textStyle` ranges in UTF-16 units; `spoken`: the same reading for speech)

## Conventions

- Signal shows Markdown as typed ("**bold**" with its asterisks). Every text goes out through `App._deliver`, which turns markup into Signal's text styles (`signal_text.styled`: **bold**, ~~strike~~, `code` and closed fences as monospace, a "# heading" line bold, "- " and "* " line starts "• ", *italic* only with clear edges; never underscores, a web address (punctuation after it is the sentence's), math such as 2*3 or 2**3, a closing marker after a slash or a backslash (a glob: "**/node_modules/**"), a lone or unbalanced marker, or markers across lines; a line where a dropped marker would sit inside a web or mail address of the result ("**evil**.com") goes out as typed) and `SignalClient.send` sends them as signal-cli's `textStyle` ("start:length:STYLE", UTF-16 code units; one bad entry fails the whole send in signal-cli, so a list that `signal_text.valid` refuses is dropped and the text goes out without). The stored text keeps its markup (history, search, a resend after a restart converts again); a conversion that fails sends the text as written; a footer goes out in italics. The guards read what the phone shows too: brain's link guards also read the text without markers (`signal_text.unmarked`) and defang or drop a link only that reading has, where it stands; `claims` reads every text as `claims.shown` (no markers, no list or heading start: "**Reminder set** (#5)" is code's own note's look); `initiative.check_text` runs each check on both forms; a calendar place loses its markers before its links. A text over 2000 UTF-8 bytes gets only the ranges inside its first 2000 bytes (signal-cli's body piece; the apps' handling past it is untested). Code-written texts use the same markup: bold headings, a blank line between sections, one item per line ("•", or the item's own number); Gemma's tool results (`mail_digest`, `list_reminders`) keep their lines, so her requests stay as they were. Speech never carries markup: `voice.spoken_text` reads through `signal_text.spoken` (a struck word is left out); links and paths are left out, but a command the user types (`voice.COMMANDS`, every one /help names) is said as "slash reminders". Three dots between two words theory's parser reads as chords are a comma (`voice._dots_between_chords`: "C... G... Am..." is "C, G, Am.", one part the voice service spells as one run; folded into a full stop, each chord was a part of its own and "G." was read "Jay", the chord speech audit, 18 Oct), and so are three dots between a chord and a joining word before another (`voice._JOINS`, and, or and then in English and the eight languages that spell chords: "G... then C" is "G, then C"; the review, 18 Oct); after or before other words they stay ("G... then the chorus"); "C...G...Am" without spaces is read the same way. A run of marks is said as its first, but a comma or a semicolon after a dotted abbreviation's last dot stays (`voice._one_mark`: "N.C., then G", "e.g., apples", and a table's "| N.C. | G |" as "N.C.; G."). `voice.sentences` ends a part at "N.C." only before a capital, as at "a.m.", and never before chords (`voice._chords_follow`: a word theory's parser reads, then another or the sentence's end: "N.C. G C D" is one part, "N.C. A good start." two; the review, 18 Oct). Her stage directions ("(pause)", "*smiles*", "(laughs)", "(pausa)", "（間）") are never spoken or sent (owner, 18 Oct): `voice.without_stage_directions` leaves out a whole bracketed group of only `language.STAGE_DIRECTIONS` and `STAGE_SOFT` words, or a starred one of `STAGE_STARRED`'s (third-person and -ing forms: "*smiles*", never "*pause*" or "*smile*"); one in lower case wherever it stands, a capitalised one only between sentences (`_between_sentences`: a title in brackets stays, "(Applause) by Lady Gaga"; known gap: German's "Also (Pause) weiter" is said); never bold ("**Beat** the eggs"), code (a fence or inline, matched first and kept) or a bracket right beside a letter or digit ("time.sleep(pause)"; review 18 Oct), keeping the punctuation around it ("Em... (pause) ... C..." is "Em... C...") and every other bracket ("C (live version)", "(x2)", a link's text); it runs in brain's filters (`_filtered`, practice replies too: the stored answer has none) and in `spoken_text`. Known gap: her own messages (`initiative`) are not filtered.

## Rules

- signal-cli runs with `--trust-new-identities on-first-use`, pinned in `signal_io.signal_cli_command` (never left to the default, never `always`); setup's own signal-cli calls pass the same. A changed safety number drops that sender's messages: signal-cli's receive `exception` (0.14.9: `{"message", "type": "UntrustedIdentityException"}` next to the envelope) reaches the bridge on its own stream (`SignalClient.problems()`, type, sender and device only, never the message, which names the account), is logged without the account and kept in `status.json` (`security.identity_changed`) until a message from that user opens again; doctor and `/status` show ✗ with the compare-then-trust steps (setup_assistant.md, 7. Operations).
- Her own Signal account's devices: counted with `listDevices` through the running JSON-RPC (`SignalClient.list_devices`: ids and creation times, never names) at the start and every 4 hours, kept in `status.json` (`security.linked_devices`); more than 1 (this Mac) is ✗ in doctor and `/status`, and one note when the count goes up.

## Gotchas

- One signal-cli process per account: stop the bridge before running signal-cli by hand.
- A photo arrives as one JSON line of base64 from signal-cli (20 MB → 27 MB): the reader limit is 32 MiB.
